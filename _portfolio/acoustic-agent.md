AcousticAgent converts structured floorplans into editable, acoustically connected multi-room scenes for distributed speech processing. Its FP-RIR dataset covers 15,376 residential floorplans.

**Research topics:** Room impulse responses, Spatial audio, Floorplan-centric simulation, Distributed microphones.
