**Zhiqi Ai**, Han Cheng, Shiyi Mu, Yongjin Zhou, Shugong Xu

AcousticAgent converts structured floorplans into editable, acoustically connected multi-room scenes. It models scene geometry, connectivity, materials, furniture, sources, and distributed microphones to generate multichannel room impulse responses and reverberant audio. The accompanying FP-RIR dataset covers 15,376 residential floorplans.

Public preprint: arXiv:2610.22936, submitted on October 16, 2026.
